**Sent shows your own mail, not the reply that came after it.** A conversation in Sent was drawn
from its newest message, so a reply someone sent you put its recipients, its text, its time and
its unread state on the row and moved the row to the top: it looked like received mail filed under
Sent. A row in Sent is now drawn from the mail you sent in that conversation, and Sent is ordered by
when you last sent. Opened, the conversation still shows every message, replies included. No
schema change.
