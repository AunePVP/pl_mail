**Bookings from genuine senders are added to the calendar on iCloud accounts too.** iCloud Mail
writes a separate Authentication-Results header for each check and names its servers under
`icloud.com` while the account is read from `imap.mail.me.com`, so no message on an iCloud account
was ever recognised as coming from its sender: every booking was offered as a card and every change
to one was held. The headers are now read as the one report they are. No schema change.
