**The AI boxes above search results and the AI search chat no longer reset by themselves.** While a
search was open, every background refresh of the list (new mail, polling, coming back to the tab)
emptied "Coming up", "Best matches" and "In short" and loaded them again, and cleared the search
chat. They are now left alone. Going back to an AI search, or reloading it, shows the conversation
it had instead of sending the question to the model again; pressing the "AI search" button still
starts a new chat. No schema change.
