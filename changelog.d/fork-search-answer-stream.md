**"Kurz gesagt" is written while you watch.** The chat model's answer above the search results now
appears word by word as the model writes it, instead of after a wait with nothing on screen; the
links and groups follow once it is finished. Leaving the page stops the model. And a question such
as "was habe ich als letztes bestellt" no longer lists every flight from Zürich in the coming-events
panel: words of a question are ignored there, and the others must start a word. No schema change.
