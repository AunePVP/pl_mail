**Ask the AI about a mail's attachments.** "Ask AI" beside the attachments opens a chat in which the
writing model reads the whole attachment and answers questions about it: amounts, deadlines, what to
do, a summary. It reads PDFs (page by page, scans as pictures), Word, Excel, PowerPoint and
OpenDocument files, text and CSV, and photos when the AI server takes pictures (OpenAI-compatible,
e.g. vLLM). Behind the "Conversation summaries" switch. No schema change.
