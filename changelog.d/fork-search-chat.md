**Search by asking.** The search box has an "AI search" button. It takes what you typed as a
question — "wann ist meine Busfahrt nach Hamburg" — and the AI works out the searches that would
find the answer, runs them, and answers from what they found, naming the mails worth opening; those
open in the reading pane. Below the answer you can ask a follow-up ("und zurück?"), and the AI keeps
the conversation in mind, searching again only when it needs to. Needs thread summaries switched on in
Admin → AI. Questions about a time — "was steht die Woche an" — are answered from the calendar
for those days. No schema change.
